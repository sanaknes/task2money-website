A straightforward reward journey

The intended flow emphasizes transparency rather than promises of easy income.

1

Create an account

Secure account access is planned for the production version.

2

Choose an eligible task

Review conditions, eligibility and any available reward before starting.

3

Partner verification

Completion is checked against provider rules and anti-fraud safeguards.

4

Reward & withdrawal

Approved rewards may become withdrawable when payment systems are enabled and conditions are met.

🏦 Bank transfer

A potential alternative for eligible verified accounts, subject to provider support and compliance checks.

Minimum withdrawal thresholds, fees, processing times and availability will be published before launch. No withdrawal functionality is active on this demo website.

Built for responsible partner integrations

We are exploring partnerships with eligible survey, offerwall, gaming and rewarded-media providers serving India.

What we aim to provide

  • Clear placement of approved campaigns and their terms
  • Consent-based user experience and transparent disclosures
  • Completion validation, duplicate-activity controls and fraud review
  • Support workflows for missing or disputed rewards

Integration principles

  • Only activate campaigns after publisher acceptance
  • Respect each provider's traffic and incentive policies
  • No misleading reward claims or fabricated performance metrics
  • Publish applicable policies before handling real user data

We do not claim an active commercial relationship with any named network at this stage.

Trust starts with clarity

🔒 Privacy by design

Collect only necessary information with appropriate notice and controls once services launch.

📄 Clear terms

Task eligibility, rejection reasons and payout conditions will be disclosed.

🛡️ Fraud prevention

Protect partners and legitimate users from duplicate or invalid completions.

🤝 Fair expectations

Rewards depend on actual approved campaigns and validated participation.

Before production launch, Task2Money will need published Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, contact/support information, secure authentication and operational payment arrangements.
